A leading · means the token starts with a space, so ·the (279) and the (1820) are different entries. Text between backslashes, like \ F0 9F 98 \, is the technical form, the same way a dictionary shows how a word is pronounced. Byte fragments are pieces of emoji and other characters that only make sense once the model snaps them together. Some tokens are invisible, like zero width spaces, or are marks meant to sit on another letter, so they’re listed by their name instead. Special tokens are signals, not words: they mark where messages start and stop, plus slots Meta saved for later.
